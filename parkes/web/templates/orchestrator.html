Pass Orchestrator

Sequences dish + SDR ownership across the satellites and downlinks managed on the Satdump page: finds the soonest pass, points the rotator at it, and launches that downlink's "app" profile for the duration. Mutually exclusive with Satdump Autotrack, SDR Network Sharing, and manual tracking -- only one can hold the dish/SDR at a time.

 

App Profiles

Maps a downlink's app name (see tracked objects) to the command run for the duration of its pass. Placeholders filled in at launch: {frequency}, {output_dir}, {source}, {source_id}, {samplerate}.
