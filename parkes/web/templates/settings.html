Rotator (rotctld)

Parkes starts and supervises rotctld itself when "Managed" is on. Model list comes from rotctl -l on this machine's Hamlib install. "Host" is always where this app connects; "Bind address" only matters when Managed is on -- it's what interface the daemon Parkes spawns listens on. Set it to 0.0.0.0 to let other rotctld clients (e.g. gpredict on a laptop) reach it over the LAN while Host stays localhost for Parkes's own connection. DTR/RTS state: rotctld defaults to clearing both on open, which on some native-USB boards (e.g. this project's ESP32-S3 EasyCommII board) is a transition the chip treats as a reset trigger -- setting "ON" makes it raise instead, matching what the OS already did on open rather than undoing it.

Firmware (ESP32)

Updates the EasyCommII board's app firmware over its USB serial connection. Build DishControll, then select the app image (e.g. Arecibo.bin, from its build/ output) below -- only the app partition is rewritten, not the bootloader or partition table. rotctld is stopped automatically for the duration of the flash (freeing the serial port) and restarted once it finishes, whether it succeeds or fails. 


      

    


    

      

        
Arecibo 

        
      

      

        Firmware calibration/limit settings, read and written directly on the board's NVS storage
        over the same USB-serial link EasyComm/rotctld use. rotctld is paused while this is
        actively in use, and handed back automatically about 30s after the last read/write here --
        opening the serial port resets the board, so it's held open across a burst of activity
        rather than reopened (and the board reset) on every single call.

GPS (gpsd) 

          
        

        
      

      

        Off by default -- most setups rely on the system's own gpsd.service, which other things

        consumer. "Host" is always where this app connects; "Bind address" only matters
        when Managed is on (anything other than localhost tells gpsd to listen on
        all interfaces, not just loopback).
